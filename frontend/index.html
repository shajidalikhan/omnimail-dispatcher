Sender Identity & SMTP Dispatcher

💾 Saved Locally to .env 🔒 Zero-Retention Active

Local Mode: Your key is saved to the .env file on your machine and auto-filled on every restart. Keep your .env file private and never share it.

Zero-Retention Security: Your key is processed purely in volatile memory during dispatch and is NEVER saved to the server disk.

How to Get Your Mail Key

Google / Gmail Guide
  1. Log in to your Google Account (myaccount.google.com).
  2. Go to the Security tab and ensure 2-Step Verification is ON.
  3. Search for "App Passwords" or visit myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  4. Name it "OmniMail Dispatcher" and click Create.
  5. Copy the generated 16-character passcode (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop) and paste it into the key box on the left.
